Tips

Tap a piece to select it, drag to move, drag the square handles to resize. Drag the dark handles on the outline to resize the whole floor. For an L-shaped room, overlap two rooms. To show that two spaces are open to each other, put an Opening (under Doors, windows, stairs) on the line between them and stretch it to the width of the gap. Keys: Delete, arrows to nudge, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+D to duplicate, R to rotate, M to mirror.
